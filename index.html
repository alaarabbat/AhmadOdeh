Greek Yogurt Protein Bowl
420 kcal P38g C32g F12g
Ingredients
  • 200g full-fat Greek yogurt
  • 1 scoop whey protein (vanilla)
  • 40g rolled oats
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 80g mixed berries
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 10g walnuts
Method
  1. Layer oats at the bottom of a deep bowl.
  2. Mix whey into yogurt until smooth; pour over oats.
  3. Top with chia seeds and berries.
  4. Drizzle honey; add walnuts last.
  5. Eat oats first, then yogurt-protein layer — blood sugar sequencing.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Start with the oats to clothe the carbs — the fat and protein in yogurt blunt the glucose spike.
🔁 Habit Tip: Keep yogurt visible on top shelf, oats beside it. 5-min assembly — zero decision fatigue.

Beef Kofta with Tabbouleh
560 kcal P44g C36g F22g
Ingredients
  • 200g lean beef mince
  • ½ onion, grated
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Salt, pepper
  • 120g bulgur wheat
  • Large bunch parsley, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • ¼ cucumber, diced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice 1 lemon
Method
  1. Mix mince, onion, garlic, spices; form 8 small kofta.
  2. Grill or pan-fry 4 min each side.
  3. Soak bulgur in boiling water 20 min; drain, cool.
  4. Combine bulgur, parsley, tomatoes, cucumber with oil and lemon.
  5. Serve kofta alongside tabbouleh — eat the tabbouleh first.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Parsley is rich in antioxidants and fibre — eat tabbouleh as your green starter before the kofta.
🔁 Habit Tip: Batch kofta and freeze raw. Cook from frozen in 12 min — perfect fridge-to-table meal.

Lamb Kofta with Fattoush
570 kcal P40g C34g F26g
Ingredients
  • 200g lamb mince
  • ½ onion grated
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • ½ tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • Salt, pepper
  • Large romaine lettuce
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • Fresh mint and parsley
  • 2 pitta breads, torn
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp sumac
  • Labneh to serve
Method
  1. Mix lamb, onion, spices; form 8 kofta around skewers.
  2. Grill 4–5 min per side.
  3. Toast pitta pieces in oven 8 min at 190°C until crispy.
  4. Toss lettuce, tomatoes, cucumber, herbs with oil and lemon.
  5. Add crispy pitta last; dust with sumac. Serve kofta alongside labneh.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Fattoush is sequenced correctly — eat the salad in full before the kofta, and the pitta last.
🔁 Habit Tip: Kofta freeze well raw. Make 20 on Sunday — weeknight meal in 10 min from frozen.

Breakfast
Turkish Egg Menemen
480 kcal P32g C18g F28g
Ingredients
  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 medium tomato, diced
  • ½ green pepper, diced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • ½ tsp paprika
  • Fresh parsley
  • 2 slices sourdough
Method
  1. Sauté onion and pepper in olive oil on medium heat for 5 min.
  2. Add tomato and spices; cook until soft (5 min).
  3. Create 3 wells; crack eggs in. Don't stir — soft scramble.
  4. Cover and cook 3–4 min until whites set, yolks soft.
  5. Serve with sourdough — eat eggs before bread.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Eat the vegetables and eggs before the sourdough. Protein and fat first = 30% lower glucose spike.
🔁 Habit Tip: Batch the tomato-pepper base Sunday evening — morning cook time drops to 7 minutes.

Asian Chicken Salad
440 kcal P40g C28g F14g
Ingredients
  • 150g chicken breast, poached
  • 100g mixed salad leaves
  • 1 carrot, julienned
  • ½ red cabbage, shredded
  • 3 spring onions
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • Fresh coriander
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp honey
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, grated
Method
  1. Poach chicken 12 min; shred with forks.
  2. Whisk dressing: vinegar, soy, sesame oil, honey, ginger.
  3. Toss leaves, carrot, cabbage with dressing.
  4. Top with chicken, spring onions, sesame, coriander.
  5. Eat the greens first before the chicken.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: The vinegar in the dressing is Inchauspé's vinegar hack — add 1 tbsp to a glass of water before this meal.
🔁 Habit Tip: Use rotisserie chicken and pre-shredded slaw mix — 6 min total.

One-Pan Chicken Thighs
530 kcal P44g C22g F28g
Ingredients
  • 2 bone-in chicken thighs (300g)
  • 300g baby potatoes, halved
  • 200g green beans
  • 1 head garlic
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 lemon, sliced
  • Fresh rosemary and thyme
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Salt, pepper
Method
  1. Preheat oven 210°C.
  2. Rub chicken with oil, paprika, salt, pepper.
  3. Arrange potatoes, beans, garlic in roasting tray; season.
  4. Place chicken skin-up on top of veg.
  5. Lay lemon slices and herbs. Roast 35 min — skin should be golden.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Eat the green beans first while the chicken rests — fibre first, then protein, then potatoes last.
🔁 Habit Tip: One tray, one wash-up. Double the recipe — reheat next day in 3 min.

Ingredients

  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • ½ tsp coriander
  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 1L vegetable stock

  • 240ml unsweetened almond milk
  • 30g vanilla whey protein

Turkey Stuffed Peppers
500 kcal P42g C34g F16g
Ingredients
  • 2 large bell peppers
  • 200g turkey mince
  • 100g cooked brown rice
  • 1 tin diced tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp Italian herbs
  • 50g mozzarella, grated
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper
  • Fresh basil
Method
  1. Halve peppers; remove seeds. Brush with oil.
  2. Sauté onion and garlic 5 min; add turkey, brown 5 min.

🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: The pepper acts as a natural fiber wrap — all carbs are inside a vegetable container.

Shakshuka with Chickpeas
460 kcal P28g C38g F18g
Ingredients
  • 3 eggs
  • 200g tinned chickpeas
  • 400g passata
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 tsp harissa
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • Fresh coriander
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
Method
  1. Heat oil; fry onion, garlic, and pepper 7 min.
  2. Add harissa, cumin, paprika — toast 1 min.
  3. Add passata and chickpeas; simmer 10 min.
  4. Make 3 wells; crack eggs in. Cover, cook 5 min.
  5. Scatter coriander and serve.
🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Chickpeas are low-GI — they clothe the tomato sugars. The eggs slow absorption further.
🔁 Habit Tip: Keep passata and chickpeas in pantry. Zero shopping needed — pantry meal.

Beef Stir-Fry with Broccoli
510 kcal P42g C32g F20g
Ingredients
  • 180g lean beef strips
  • 200g broccoli florets
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 spring onions
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tsp cornstarch
  • 120g cooked jasmine rice
  • Sesame seeds
Method
  1. Marinate beef in soy, cornstarch, garlic, ginger 10 min.
  2. Blanch broccoli 2 min; set aside.

🩸 Blood Sugar Tip: Broccoli is Dr. Rhonda Patrick's sulforaphane superfood — eat it before the rice for optimal glucose response.
